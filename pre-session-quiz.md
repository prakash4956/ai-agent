# Four questions before the session

Answer the four questions below in your own words, one or two sentences each. There is no
mark; the answers show the instructor what the room already thinks, and the session returns
to each question. Send your four answers to the instructor in a direct message in the class
channel at least two days before the session.

1. When an agent searches the web, who runs the search, the model or the program? Answer
   in one sentence.
2. At step 3 of a run, what does the model remember of step 1, and why?
3. Name the three settings your chatbot read from `.env`.
4. An agent's report says that a page states a fact. Write one sentence on how you would
   check that.
